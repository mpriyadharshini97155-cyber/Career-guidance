🎤 Interview Preparation

Prepare for HR and Technical Interviews.

Introduce yourself confidently in 1–2 minutes.

Explain your strengths with examples.

Mention a real weakness and how you are improving it.

Explain your skills, projects and enthusiasm.

Revise programming, core subjects and projects.

Practice logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude and verbal ability.

Improve communication and teamwork skills.

Dress professionally, be confident and maintain eye contact.
