🏢 Placement Preparation

Prepare yourself for campus placements.

Practice quantitative aptitude, reasoning and verbal ability.

Revise programming, core subjects and important concepts.

Practice coding problems on online platforms regularly.

Create a clear and professional resume.

Practice technical and HR interviews with friends or mentors.

Improve English speaking and presentation skills.

Learn about the company's products, services and interview process.

Be confident, arrive on time and answer questions honestly.
