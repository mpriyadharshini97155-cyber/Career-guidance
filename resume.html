📄 Resume Builder Guide

Create a professional resume for jobs and internships.

Name, phone number, email and LinkedIn profile.

Add your degree, college, CGPA and passing year.

Include technical and soft skills relevant to your career.

Mention your college projects and personal projects.

Add NPTEL, Coursera and other certifications.

Include internship experience and responsibilities.
